LB & Mediccy Pharmacy

RoleDigital Marketing Officer
DurationFeb–May 2026
PlatformsInstagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook
LocationLagos, Nigeria

The brief

Two retail pharmacy brands, one marketer. Grow both audiences and turn social attention into real orders, without ever talking down other pharmacies.

What I did

  • 01Built a six-pillar content calendar and ran it in rotating cycles: Monday carousel, TikTok and LinkedIn post, Wednesday reel, Friday flyer and TikTok.
  • 02Wrote every script for the on-camera pharmacists, grounded in Nigerian health habits: the “I'll manage” mindset, agbo versus medication, men's health stigma.
  • 03Launched both brands on LinkedIn from zero with weekly thought-leadership posts.
  • 04Produced a nine-post partner series featuring eight health partners, including Leadway HMO and Wella Health.
  • 05Sent weekly and cumulative performance reports to the Executive Director, including sales by channel and branch.
