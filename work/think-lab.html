THINK Lab Africa

RoleContent & Growth Manager
DurationJul–Oct 2026
PlatformsInstagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, WhatsApp
ProductTHINK Excel course

The brief

A premium Excel course with no launch date and no audience of its own. The content had to justify the price and pull people into registration.

What I did

  • 01Ran THINK Excel content on the founder's personal account first, then moved it to a dedicated THINK Lab Africa page and wrote its bio.
  • 02Tied every post to a live registration form, so signups became the conversion metric.
  • 03Built the weekly mix: a Monday teaching video, then gesture-only POV videos on Wednesday and Friday that took minutes to film.
  • 04Wrote LinkedIn thought-leadership pieces with a different angle from each week's video.
  • 05Set up and ran the WhatsApp community: weekly THINK Thursday challenges plus social days to keep members talking.
