About

I plan, write and ship content that gets small brands seen and remembered.

I'm Aizenosa, a content and digital marketing strategist from Benin City. I've run social for pharmacy chains, an NGO, an Excel training brand and a payments startup, usually more than one at once.

My work covers the full loop: pillars and calendars, verbatim video scripts, captions, carousels I design myself, editing, posting, and the weekly numbers that show what worked. I studied English and Literature at the University of Benin, so the writing is where I start.
